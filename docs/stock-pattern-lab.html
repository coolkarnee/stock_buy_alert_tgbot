Stock Pattern Lab

A transparent alert-only scanner for Momentum BUY and Episodic Pivot BUY signals, with explicit swing-strength and portfolio-risk rules.

Momentum BUY

Every live strength, breakout, liquidity, opening-range, swing-period, and SL-risk condition must pass.

Episodic Pivot BUY

An independent five-condition alert for a gap above 5%, price above developing EMA21, price above ₹20, ATR qualification, and liquidity.

Swing performance

Every alert uses the previous trading day's low as the initial SL. A +10% move in Bear/Choppy context or +15% in Bull context books part of the position; the remainder trails on daily 10 EMA/breakeven, then 20 EMA. Rows are marked as Trade open, Partial exit, or Full exit through the report date.

When will a Telegram BUY alert fire?

This section explains the alert from a share-market perspective, without requiring technical knowledge of the implementation.

Momentum BUY

This alert means the stock is showing a broad combination of strength rather than only a short-lived price spike. It suggests that the stock is trending strongly, buyers are participating, the price is attempting to move beyond an important recent range, and the stock remains reasonably close to its recent high with enough liquidity for meaningful participation.

In simple terms, the stock is demonstrating sustained market strength and attempting to continue that move. It is not a guarantee that the stock will rise or a recommendation to buy without checking risk, news, valuation, position size, and the wider market.

Episodic Pivot BUY

This alert means the stock opened with a sharp positive gap and is continuing to hold above an important trend level. This can indicate new information, a result, an announcement, or a sudden change in investor demand.

Gap-up stocks can be volatile: they may continue higher, move sideways, or quickly give back the gap. The alert is therefore a prompt for further research and risk planning, not confirmation of a guaranteed return.

The message can arrive only during the live monitoring session after the market opens. Every required condition for that category must be satisfied at the same time. At most one Momentum BUY and one Episodic Pivot BUY is sent for the same stock during a run. No automatic order is placed.

Telegram delivery requires the Telegram settings. Without them, the alert appears in the workflow log.

Previous-day-low risk sizing

Momentum BUY alerts are allowed only when the previous-day-low SL is no more than 6% below entry. The maximum position is capped at 25% of the portfolio.

SL distance % = ((Buy Price − Previous Day Low) / Buy Price) × 100
Position Size % = min(25%, 1% / SL distance)

Example: a 5% SL distance gives a 20% maximum position. A 4% distance reaches the 25% cap. EP alerts do not use this filter.

Episodic Pivot BUY

  • Day's open is strictly more than 5% above yesterday's close.
  • Current price is above developing EMA21.
  • Price is strictly above ₹20.
  • ATR is above 3.9%, except IPOs.
  • Turnover/liquidity is above ₹20 Cr.

EP alerts do not require Momentum BUY volume confirmation, momentum, breakout, rally-strength, near-high, or opening-range conditions.

Opening-range mode

The default in config/settings.py is OPENING_RANGE_BREAKOUT_MODE="close". Close mode requires a completed intraday candle close above the opening-range high. Setting it to "spot" triggers when live price crosses above the range high.

Market context

The live alert displays one of exactly three categories:

  • Bull Market: benchmark above both 10 and 20 EMA, with both EMA slopes rising.
  • Bear Market: benchmark below both 10 and 20 EMA.
  • Choppy Market: all other conditions.

The scanner uses the exact NIFTY 500 Upstox index record for stock-alert market context when available. Momentum BUY alerts also require the previous-day-low SL to be no more than 6% below entry; their maximum allocation for 1% portfolio risk is capped at 25%. EP alerts are not subject to this position-size filter. The open and close summaries use exact Upstox records only; unavailable values are shown as NA and the message reports how many of the six indices were fetched.

Persistent performance sheet

After the live alert workflow and daily performance workflow complete, the swing workflow recalculates all stored alerts and commits the latest file to artifacts/swing_performance.csv. It also uploads a downloadable swing-performance-latest artifact and sends a Telegram notification when a new Partial exit or Full exit occurs today. The Excel file has an overall dashboard on its first tab and weekly sections on the second tab; each week has a merged centered title, weekly numbering, and repeated headers/descriptions.
