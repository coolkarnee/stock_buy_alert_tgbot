STOCK PATTERN LAB - BUY ALERT RULES

1. MOMENTUM BUY

A broad strength-and-breakout signal. All required checks
must qualify together:

- Price above developing EMA21 after 21 completed trading sessions; earlier sessions are warm-up.
- Price above the previous trading day's close.
- Price above the previous-session breakout reference,
  including a 0.50% buffer.
- Price strictly above Rs 20.
- Turnover/liquidity strictly above Rs 20 Cr.
- Within 15% of the 52-week high before 63 completed sessions; within 20% thereafter.
- ATR >3.49% after 14 ATR inputs are available; earlier sessions are warm-up.
- Opening-range breakout confirmed on at least one of:
  3m, 5m, 15m, 30m or 60m.
- Previous-day-low stop distance must be positive and
  no more than 8% below the alert price.

At least one activity confirmation is required:
- RVOL >1.30x; OR
- Pocket pivot; OR
- First-30-minute volume surge; OR
- Price up at least 4% from today's open; OR
- Today's cumulative volume >9 million shares.

After 63 completed sessions, Momentum also needs at least one
swing-strength move:
1W >10%, 1M >19%, 3M >29%, 6M >49%,
1Y >99%, 18M >149%, or 24M >199%.

Maximum indicated position size is capped at 25%,
using a 1% portfolio-risk calculation.

IPO universe membership uses actual listing date within
IPO_LOOKBACK_MONTHS (default 12 months). This setting does not
change alert exemptions; live warm-ups use completed session age.


2. EPISODIC PIVOT BUY - EP BUY

An independent opening-gap signal:

- Today's opening gap strictly above 4.5% versus the
  previous trading day's close.
- Pre-open IEP above the gap threshold nominates a
  candidate; IEP is indicative, not a traded price.
- After 09:15 IST, a fresh regular-session quote confirms
  the actual opening gap. EMA21 is required after 21 completed
  sessions; earlier sessions are EMA warm-up.
- Price strictly above Rs 20.
- Turnover/liquidity strictly above Rs 20 Cr.
- ATR strictly above EP_MIN_ATR_PCT (default 3.0%) after
  14 ATR inputs are available; earlier sessions are warm-up.

No Momentum ORB, volume-surge, swing-strength, near-high
or 8% stop-distance requirement.

The live monitor connects at 09:00 IST to collect pre-open
IEP candidates. Stocks without usable IEP can still be
detected by the regular post-open completed-candle scan.


3. EP RADAR OPENING-RANGE BUY

A separate scan for stocks whose verified opening gap
is strictly above 4.5%. A normal EP BUY is not required.

Five opening ranges are tracked independently:
3 minutes, 5 minutes, 15 minutes, 30 minutes and 1 hour.

ORB confirmation follows the same choice as Momentum:
- CLOSE - completed candle closes strictly above the
  opening-range high.
- SPOT - live price is strictly above the high after
  the opening range has formed.

Current configuration: CLOSE.
Close confirmation uses completed one-minute candles;
it does not wait for another full range-sized candle.

The qualifying price must also be:
- Above developing EMA21 after 21 completed sessions; before
  then EMA21 is in warm-up.
- Strictly above Rs 20.
- Supported by turnover/liquidity above Rs 20 Cr using available
  history, up to the 50-session average.

No additional Momentum volume-surge, ATR, swing-strength,
near-high or 8% stop-distance requirement.

Each range can alert once per stock per monitoring run:
up to five EP-radar alerts.

The 30-minute range covers 09:15 through 09:44 IST.
Earliest confirmation: spot after 09:45, close around 09:46,
subject to data availability and the other radar conditions.


COMMON NOTES

- Every BUY alert includes NIFTY 500 trend and stocks-above-200-MA breadth.
- NIFTY 500 Uptrending: above EMA10 and EMA20, both rising.
- NIFTY 500 Downtrending: below EMA10 and EMA20, both falling.
- Otherwise NIFTY 500 is Chopping.
- Broader Bull: >50% above 200 MA. Broader Bear: <=50%.
- Oversold: <=20% above 200 MA. Overbought: >=80%.
- Matching Uptrending/Broader Bull gives Bull Market; matching
  Downtrending/Broader Bear gives Bear Market. Chopping is
  Choppy Market; conflicting readings are Mixed Market.
- Missing data is shown as NA while retaining any available
  NIFTY or breadth value. Live breadth is NA until fetched;
  available breadth is shown with its observation date.
- Swing reports retain both readings. Only Bull Market uses
  the +15% / 20% profit-booking rule; other contexts use +10% / 30%.
- Momentum BUY, EP BUY and EP-radar alerts are independent.
  The same stock can receive all three categories.
- Momentum and normal EP BUY each alert once per stock
  per monitoring run.
- Every stock alert includes Sector; unavailable data
  appears as NA and does not block the signal.
- Intraday Alert Count is the number of live alert events for
  the stock on the current IST date; it resets next day.
- Daily Alert Count is the number of distinct sessions with
  any alert in the latest 7 trading days. Multiple same-day
  EP, Momentum, and radar alerts count as one alert-day.
- If the session calendar is unavailable, Daily Alert Count
  is NA. The count includes only recorded signal events.
- The live scanner persists completed NIFTY 500 session dates
  in artifacts/trading_sessions.csv for this calculation.
- Market-breadth trend compares the latest 7 stored trading
  sessions for stocks above 20/50/200-day MAs. It is marked
  provisional with 2-6 sessions and unavailable below 2;
  a full trend needs 7 successful session records.
- These counts measure recorded signals, not guaranteed
  Telegram deliveries or a proven increase in win probability.
  History starts when tracking is deployed; older events may
  be unavailable.
- Turnover is the scanner's historical-average liquidity
  measure including today's developing data - not a
  requirement for Rs 20 Cr to have traded today alone.
- Performance uses the first recorded alert price/time
  per stock per trading day. Later signals do not replace it.
- End-of-day sector counts include all signal events.
- Swing CSV and Excel keep Sector as a separate column and
  show Intraday Alert Count and Daily Alert Count (7 Trading Days).
- The Excel Dashboard groups outcomes by entry market:
  Bull, Bear, Choppy, Mixed, NA, or Unknown. Win/loss/breakeven and
  win rate use fully exited trades with usable returns only.
  Open/partial counts and all-trade average returns are
  shown separately; averages are not portfolio P&L.
- Previous trading day's low is shown as the initial stop.
- Data/provider delays can delay alerts.
- Broker API requests are paced to stay within per-process
  quotas. Large watchlists can delay startup and candle scans.
  Do not run multiple scanners/reports on the same broker
  account at once; their separate quotas are not coordinated.
- Long Telegram messages split automatically without losing
  signal details. Long image/document captions follow as text.
  Rate limits may delay delivery; network failures can still
  prevent it.
- Restarting the monitor can repeat same-day signals.
- Alerts do not place orders or guarantee returns.
