When will a Telegram BUY alert fire?

This section explains the alert from a share-market perspective, without requiring technical knowledge of the implementation.

Momentum BUY

This alert means the stock is showing a broad combination of strength rather than only a short-lived price spike. It suggests that the stock is trending strongly, buyers are participating, the price is attempting to move beyond an important recent range, and the stock remains reasonably close to its recent high with enough liquidity for meaningful participation.

In simple terms, the stock is demonstrating sustained market strength and attempting to continue that move. It is not a guarantee that the stock will rise or a recommendation to buy without checking risk, news, valuation, position size, and the wider market.

Episodic Pivot BUY

This alert means the stock opened with a sharp positive gap and is continuing to hold above an important trend level. This can indicate new information, a result, an announcement, or a sudden change in investor demand.

Gap-up stocks can be volatile: they may continue higher, move sideways, or quickly give back the gap. The alert is therefore a prompt for further research and risk planning, not confirmation of a guaranteed return.

The live monitor connects at 09:00 IST to collect indicative pre-open IEP candidates. IEP is not a traded price; EP BUY is sent only after a fresh regular-session quote confirms the required conditions. At most one Momentum BUY and one Episodic Pivot BUY is sent for the same stock during a run. No automatic order is placed.

Telegram delivery requires the Telegram settings. Without them, the alert appears in the workflow log.

Long Telegram messages are split automatically rather than shortened, including the signal-frequency information. Oversized image or workbook captions follow as text. Broker requests are paced within per-process quotas, so large watchlists can delay startup and candle confirmations; avoid running other scanners or reports on the same broker account simultaneously. Network and provider failures can still prevent delivery.

EP BUY never suppresses Momentum BUY: the same stock can receive both when their own conditions pass. The separate EP radar scan can add one breakout message per range, up to five per stock per session.

IPO_LOOKBACK_MONTHS (default 12) controls universe membership only. Live warm-ups apply to every stock based on completed sessions: ATR after 14 inputs, EMA21 after 21 sessions, and Momentum swing strength/20% near-high rules after 63 sessions. Previous-day-low risk sizing

Momentum BUY alerts are allowed only when the previous-day-low SL is no more than 8% below entry. The maximum position is capped at 25% of the portfolio.

SL distance % = ((Buy Price − Previous Day Low) / Buy Price) × 100
Position Size % = min(25%, 1% / SL distance)

Example: a 5% SL distance gives a 20% maximum position. A 4% distance reaches the 25% cap. EP alerts do not use this filter.

Episodic Pivot BUY

  • Day's open is strictly more than 4.5% above the previous trading day's close.
  • Pre-open IEP strictly more than 4.5% above yesterday's close nominates a candidate; IEP is indicative, not a traded price.
  • After 09:15 IST, a fresh regular-session quote confirms the actual opening gap. Price must be above developing EMA21 after 21 completed sessions; earlier sessions are warm-up.
  • Price is strictly above ₹20.
  • ATR is strictly above EP_MIN_ATR_PCT (default 3.0%) after 14 ATR inputs are available; earlier sessions are warm-up.
  • Turnover/liquidity is above ₹20 Cr.

EP alerts do not require Momentum BUY volume confirmation, momentum, breakout, rally-strength, near-high, or opening-range conditions.

The scanner connects at 09:00 IST and evaluates pre-open IEP candidates using actual regular-session quotes after the open. A candidate can alert later if an active EMA21, ATR, or liquidity check only passes later. Stocks without usable pre-open IEP can still be detected by the regular post-open completed-candle scan.

EP radar: a simpler gap-and-breakout watch

Every stock with a verified opening gap strictly above 4.5% enters this radar, even if the full EP BUY conditions fail. Its opening 3-minute, 5-minute, 15-minute, 30-minute and 1-hour highs become five separate reference levels.

The radar uses the same ORB confirmation choice as Momentum: close waits for a completed candle close strictly above the range high; spot uses the live price strictly above the high after the range forms. A touch or equal price is not enough. Price must be above developing EMA21 after 21 completed sessions; earlier sessions are EMA warm-up. Price >Rs 20 and turnover >Rs 20 Cr apply from the first session, using available history. Other Momentum filters are not required for this scan. Missing opening candles must become available before a range can qualify.

The current default is close confirmation using completed one-minute candles, not a requirement to wait for another full 1-hour candle. Spot confirmation does not wait for a breakout candle to close. Each of the five ranges alerts once per stock per session. Full EP and Momentum BUY alerts stay independent.

The 30-minute range forms from 09:15 through 09:44 IST. Its earliest spot confirmation is after 09:45; its earliest close confirmation is around 09:46, subject to data availability and the other radar conditions.

End-of-day review: where did today's alerts come from?

Every live BUY alert shows Intraday Alert Count (all recorded alert events for the stock today; resets next IST day) and Daily Alert Count (distinct sessions with one or more alerts among the latest seven trading days). EP, Momentum, and each radar timeframe count separately toward the intraday total; multiple events on one session count as one daily alert day. If the trading-session calendar is unavailable, Daily Alert Count is NA. These are recorded signals, not winning-trade counts.

Daily and swing returns use the first alert's buy price and time for each stock that day. Later EP, Momentum, or radar messages are confirmations, not extra trades or replacement entry prices. If Momentum arrives first, its entry is used; if EP or a radar breakout arrives first, that entry is used.

After stock-by-stock performances, the daily report groups all signal events by sector. Each sector shows total alerts, Momentum BUY alerts, EP alerts, distinct stocks, and EP radar ORB alerts, including events whose price-performance data is unavailable.

Opening-range mode

By default, Momentum requires a completed candle to close above an opening-range high, rather than simply touching or briefly crossing it. An optional spot mode uses the live price crossing instead. EP does not need this opening-range breakout.

Market context

Every live BUY alert (Momentum, EP, and EP radar ORB) includes the NIFTY 500 trend and the percentage of stocks above the 200-day moving average, with the breadth observation date when available.

  • Uptrending: NIFTY 500 is above EMA10 and EMA20, and both EMAs are rising.
  • Downtrending: NIFTY 500 is below EMA10 and EMA20, and both EMAs are falling.
  • Chopping: all other NIFTY 500 conditions.
  • Broader Bull: more than 50% of stocks are above their 200-day MA; Broader Bear: 50% or fewer.
  • Oversold: 20% or fewer above their 200-day MA; Overbought: 80% or more.

Bull Market means NIFTY 500 is Uptrending and breadth is Broader Bull; Bear Market means it is Downtrending and breadth is Broader Bear. NIFTY 500 chopping is Choppy Market; disagreement between the index and breadth is Mixed Market. Missing inputs display NA while retaining the other available reading. Context is informational and does not block BUY alerts. Live breadth is populated after the market-open TradingView scrape; earlier alerts show Stocks above 200 MA: NA.

The market-breadth alert tracks the 20-day, 50-day, and 200-day moving-average percentages. Its trend compares the latest seven recorded trading sessions; with 2–6 sessions it is marked provisional, and with fewer than two it says trend unavailable with the number of sessions recorded. A workflow run tomorrow after the first stored session can therefore show a provisional 2/7 trend; the full trend requires seven successful trading-session records.

Persistent performance sheet

The Excel Dashboard compares outcomes by the combined market context at entry: Bull, Bear, Choppy, Mixed, NA and Unknown. The detailed NIFTY trend and breadth percentage remain in each trade's Market Context field. Only a combined Bull Market entry uses the +15% / 20% profit booking rule; other, mixed, or unavailable contexts use +10% / 30%.

Sector remains a separate trade column. Two count columns show Intraday Alert Count and Daily Alert Count (7 Trading Days) at the first entry. They include later same-day confirmations without changing the first entry price; legacy entries without session-calendar coverage show NA for the daily count.

The swing report follows the first alert per stock per trading day beyond entry. Later same-day signals do not create extra swing rows or change the entry. The Excel workbook is sent to Telegram with an overall dashboard and weekly trade sections. Every trade row includes its sector, first entry price, previous-day-low stop, latest close, performance, and status. Newly recorded partial or full exits are also announced with sector context.

In Bear or Choppy entry context, reaching +10% books 30% of the position; in Bull context, reaching +15% books 20%. The remaining position is monitored using breakeven and daily closing-basis EMA rules. A 10 EMA close break exits half of the remaining size; a 20 EMA close break or breakeven break exits the remainder. These are retrospective calculations, not executed orders, and actual trading results may differ.

Recorded sector labels stay with the alert. Older entries without an sector are filled where the provider has a classification; those backfilled labels describe the company now, not necessarily at the historic entry date.

Commodity futures workflow

The live workflow also runs an isolated MCX futures scanner for GOLDTEN, SILVERMIC, CRUDEOILM, and NATGASMINI. It chooses the nearest active contract and rolls to the next expiry when the front contract has 15 days or fewer remaining.

Commodity daily context uses EMA10 and EMA20. A daily long alert requires the EMA10 crossover above EMA20 with the close above both; a short alert is the inverse. The original EMA_PULLBACK setup requires 30-minute alignment with the daily direction: long entries may undercut/touch the EMA zone and close back above it; short entries reject the zone from below. The starting tolerance is 0.25% and is configurable.

The technical layer also supports VOLATILITY_BREAKOUT and MEAN_REVERSION. Directional breakouts use the prior 20 completed 30-minute candles, a commodity-specific ADX threshold, and volume at least 1.20 times the prior 20-candle average. Rising open interest is required when usable Upstox OI is available. Starting ADX values are GOLDTEN 22, SILVERMIC 25, CRUDEOILM 25, and NATGASMINI 28. Mean reversion is allowed only with neutral daily context, ADX below the configured threshold, and a 1.5 ATR extension beyond EMA20/Bollinger extremes followed by a reversal candle. News data and time-of-day filters are not used yet, and the still-forming 30-minute candle is excluded.

Every commodity alert explicitly shows SL: the entry-day low for long signals and the entry-day high for short signals, including the percentage distance. Alerts remain informational and do not place orders, calculate position size, or set targets.
